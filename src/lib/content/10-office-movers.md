## Office relocation without losing working days

For a business, the moving cost isn't the biggest cost. Lost days are. Every day your team can't work, or your phones and systems are down, costs more than the move.

So we plan office moves around your working week. We pack and move after closing time, overnight or over the weekend, and set the new office up so people can sit down and start work.

Hamdan Movers and Packers has been moving offices, shops and clinics across the UAE for more than six years. We handle the planning, the labelling, the heavy lifting and the setup.

---

## What our office movers handle

### Workstations and office furniture
Desks, workstations, partitions, meeting tables, chairs and cabinets. We dismantle what needs dismantling and refit it at the new office.

### IT equipment
Computers, monitors, printers, servers and networking gear are packed in padded boxes and moved separately from the heavy furniture. Your IT team or provider handles the disconnecting and reconnecting. We handle the safe packing, moving and placing.

### Files and documents
Files are boxed in order and sealed, so they arrive the same way they left. Confidential files stay sealed until you open them.

### Labelling system
Every box and every piece of furniture gets a label for its new floor, room and desk. Your staff find their things on their own desk the next morning.

### Meeting rooms, kitchens and receptions
We move the reception desk, the boardroom table, the pantry fridge and the coffee machine.

### Storage
Need to store surplus furniture or archive boxes? We offer short and long-term storage.

## Night and weekend office moves

Most office moves we do start after 6 pm or on a Friday or Saturday. That way you close on the last working day in the old office and open on the next working day in the new one.

For bigger offices we split the move into phases. One department moves at a time, so the business keeps running.

---

## Building permits and certificates

Most commercial towers in Dubai, including those in DMCC, JLT, Business Bay, DIFC and Dubai Internet City, ask movers for a work permit, a gate pass or a certificate of insurance before they let trucks in. Some also limit moves to after hours.

We supply our insurance details for the building at no extra charge. Ask your facilities management what they need, and we'll work with them on the paperwork and the time slot.

## How we plan an office move

**1. Free survey.** We visit your current office, look at the furniture, IT and files, and check access at both buildings.

**2. Fixed quote and move plan.** You get a fixed price, a timeline, and a plan for which areas move when.

**3. Labelling and packing.** We bring boxes and labels ahead of time. Staff pack their personal desk items, and we pack the rest.

**4. Move day (or night).** The crew dismantles, loads, moves and unloads, following the labels.

**5. Set-up.** Workstations are rebuilt and furniture is placed according to your floor plan, so staff can start work.

## Questions about office moving

### How much do office movers in Dubai cost?
It depends on the number of workstations, the amount of furniture and equipment, and the access at both buildings. We give you a fixed quote after a free survey.

### Can you move our office at night or on the weekend?
Yes. Most of our office moves happen after hours or on Fridays and Saturdays, so the business doesn't lose working days.

### Do you move servers and computers?
Yes. We pack and move IT equipment carefully, separate from heavy furniture. Your IT team or provider should disconnect and reconnect systems.

### Do you dismantle and rebuild workstations?
Yes. Workstations, desks, partitions and cabinets are dismantled, moved and refitted at the new office.

### Can you give our building a certificate of insurance?
Yes. We provide our insurance details for your building at no extra charge.

### How long does an office move take?
A small office often moves in one night. Larger offices may move over a weekend or in phases. We agree the timeline with you before the move.

Our moves are insured. If anything is damaged while we're moving it, we'll repair it, replace it, or compensate you.

Cash, card or bank transfer.
