## Why villa moves need a different plan

A 1 BHK move is a few hours of work. A 5 bedroom villa is a different job. There's a majlis, a maid's room, a full kitchen, a storeroom, kids' rooms, a garden set, sometimes a gym. Things are on two or three floors, and some of the furniture is built to stay where it is.

If a mover sends the same small crew they'd send to a flat, the job runs into a second day nobody planned for.

Our villa movers start with a visit. We walk through the villa with you, count the rooms and the heavy pieces, check the stairs and the access, and agree a crew size and timing before we quote. Then the move runs to that plan.

## What our villa moving service includes

### A survey before the price
We visit the villa (or do a video walk-through), list the big items, and give you a fixed price.

### Crew and trucks sized for the job
More rooms means more people. We send enough movers and trucks to finish on the agreed day.

### Room-by-room packing
Each room is packed and labelled with its new room name. Kitchens, wardrobes and fragile items get their own boxes and extra wrapping.

### Heavy and awkward items
Large dining tables, majlis seating, king beds, wardrobes, gym equipment, pianos and pool tables. We take apart what comes apart and pad the rest.

### Outdoor and garden furniture
Outdoor sofas, dining sets, swings, BBQs and planters, cleaned of loose sand and wrapped before loading.

### Setting up the new villa
We unload room by room, refit the furniture, and place everything where you want it.

## Things to sort out before a villa move

**Gate passes and permits.** Most gated communities need a gate pass or move permit for our trucks. Ask your community management a few days before. We'll tell you what they usually need.

**Light fittings and wall units.** We can take down mirrors, wall art and TVs. Chandeliers and anything wired into the wall need an electrician to disconnect first.

**Pools, plants and outdoor items.** Tell us about large planters, garden furniture and outdoor majlis sets when you call, so we bring enough people and the right truck.

**Two-day moves.** For big villas, we can pack on day one and move on day two, so you're not living in boxes for a week.
