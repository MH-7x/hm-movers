## East coast moves, planned properly

Fujairah is on the other side of the mountains from the rest of the UAE. That changes how a move works. The drive is longer, crews need an early start, and a mover who hasn't planned the day can end up unloading in the dark.

Our crews come over from our base in Sharjah, which is roughly a 90-minute drive. So we plan Fujairah moves around that. We load early, keep your things on one truck, and set up the new place the same day for most apartment moves.

We take local moves inside Fujairah as well, not just moves in and out of the emirate. Hamdan Movers and Packers has been doing this work across the UAE for more than six years.

## What to plan for in Fujairah

**Book a day or two ahead.** Same-day Fujairah moves are possible when a truck is free, but booking a day or two ahead is the safest way to get the date you want.

**Early starts.** For moves to or from the west coast, we like to load in the morning. That way the drive through the mountains happens in daylight and we finish setting up the same day.

**Big homes with outdoor items.** If you have garden furniture, an outdoor majlis or a lot of heavy items, tell us when you call. We'll send enough people and the right truck.

**Summer heat.** From June to September, early morning starts are better for your things and the crew.
