## What to plan for in Ajman

**Busy towers at month end.** Al Nuaimiya, Al Rashidiya and Emirates City have a lot of tenants moving in and out at the same time, especially in the last days of the month. Lifts and parking get busy. Book a few days ahead if you can, and we'll plan the arrival time around it.

**Lift bookings and building rules.** Some towers, mostly the newer ones and those on the corniche, ask for a lift booking or a move permit from the building management. Check with your building a few days before. We'll tell you what they usually ask for.

**Big furniture in small lifts.** In many older Ajman buildings the lift is too small for a wardrobe or a king bed. We take them apart, carry them down safely, and fit them again at the new place.

**Summer heat.** From June to September we start early, before it gets too hot.
