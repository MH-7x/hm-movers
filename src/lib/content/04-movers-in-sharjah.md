## A few things that make Sharjah moves different

**Older buildings, smaller lifts.** Many buildings in Rolla, Al Nabba, Abu Shagara and Al Qasimia have small lifts or narrow stairs. We take big wardrobes and beds apart so they go down without damage, and put them together again at the other end.

**Parking is tight in the busy areas.** In Al Nahda, Al Taawun and Al Majaz, finding a spot for a truck can take time. We plan the arrival time and where the truck will stop before the day.

**Building rules.** Some towers ask for a move permit or a lift booking from the building management. Check with your building a few days before. We'll tell you what they usually ask for.

**Summer moves.** From June to September we start early in the morning, before the heat peaks.
