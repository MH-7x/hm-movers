## What to plan for in Ras Al Khaimah

**Start early.** Longer drives mean we like to load in the morning. For moves to or from Dubai, an early start also avoids traffic as you get closer to the city.

**Book a day or two ahead if you can.** Same-day RAK moves are possible when a truck is free. But a day or two of notice is the safest way to get the date and time you want.

**Gated communities and towers.** Al Hamra Village, Mina Al Arab and the Al Marjan Island towers usually ask for a gate pass, a move permit or a lift booking. Ask your community or building management a few days before. We'll tell you what they usually need.

**Villas with outdoor furniture.** If you have garden sets, swings, BBQs or a majlis outside, tell us when you call. It helps us send the right crew and truck.
