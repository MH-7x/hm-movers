## Your apartment moving checklist

**When you sign the new lease**
- Book your movers and confirm the date
- Ask both buildings for a move-out and move-in permit (often called an NOC) and book the service lifts
- Check if your building only allows moves at certain times or on certain days

**One week before**
- Arrange your Ejari, and your DEWA move-out and move-in
- Ask your landlord or agent when the move-out inspection is, so you can get your deposit back
- Clear out anything you don't want to take

**The day before**
- Defrost the fridge and freezer
- Confirm the lift booking time with building security
- Keep documents, keys, chargers and medicines in a separate bag

**On moving day**
- Meet our supervisor at the lift
- Check each room and cupboard before the truck leaves
- Return keys and access cards to the building

## Questions about moving an apartment

### How much do apartment movers in Dubai cost?
A studio starts from AED 750, a 1 BHK from AED 1,100 and a 2 BHK from AED 1,600. You get a fixed price before the move.

### Do I need a permit to move out of my apartment?
In most Dubai towers, yes. Building management usually needs a move-out or move-in permit (often called an NOC) and a service lift booking. Ask them a few days before your move.

### How long does it take to move a 1 BHK?
Usually half a day, including packing, moving and setting up. It depends on the lift access at both buildings.

### What if my lift slot is short?
Tell us when you book. We can send more people, or pack the day before so moving day is only loading and unloading.

### Can you move my flat on a weekend?
Yes, we work every day. Some buildings don't allow moves on certain days, so check with your building first.

### Will you protect the building's lifts and corridors?
We wrap furniture properly so it doesn't knock the lift walls or corridors. Some buildings also ask for lift protection, and we'll follow their rules.

### What if something gets damaged?
Our moves are insured. If anything is damaged while we're moving it, we'll repair it, replace it, or compensate you.

### How do I pay?
Cash, card or bank transfer.
