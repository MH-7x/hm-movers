## What our house movers do

### Pack every room
We pack the kitchen, living room, bedrooms, wardrobes, bathrooms, storeroom and balcony. Each box is labelled with the room it goes to, so unpacking is quicker.

### Protect what breaks
Glasses, plates, mirrors, TVs and decor get extra wrapping and proper boxes. Mattresses and sofas get covers. Wooden furniture is wrapped so it doesn't get scratched.

### Take apart and refit furniture
Beds, wardrobes, dining tables, bunk beds and kids' furniture are dismantled, moved, and fitted again. We bag the screws and fittings with each piece so nothing goes missing.

### Move it on a covered truck
Your things travel in a covered truck with a trained crew, not on an open pickup.

### Unpack and set up
At the new house we unload, rebuild the furniture, and place everything where you want it. You can sleep in a made bed on the first night.

## A simple house moving checklist

**Two weeks before**
- Book your movers and confirm the date
- Ask both buildings or communities about move permits, gate passes and lift bookings
- Start clearing things you don't want to take

**One week before**
- Arrange your DEWA (or SEWA, ADDC, FEWA) move-out and move-in
- Update your address with your bank and any deliveries
- Use up food in the freezer

**The day before**
- Defrost and dry the fridge and freezer
- Ask a plumber to disconnect the washing machine if needed
- Keep a small bag with documents, chargers, medicines and a change of clothes

**On moving day**
- Walk through each room with our supervisor
- Point out anything fragile or valuable
- Check every room and cupboard before the truck leaves

A studio starts from AED 750, a 2 BHK from AED 1,600, and a 3 bedroom villa or townhouse from AED 3,000. You get a fixed price before the move.

### How long does a house move take?
A studio or 1 BHK usually takes half a day. A 2 or 3 BHK takes about a day. Large villas take one to two days.
