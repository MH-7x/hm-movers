## Building permits and service lifts in Dubai

Most Dubai towers and many communities need a move-out or move-in permit (often called an NOC) from building management. They usually ask you to book the service lift as well. If you skip this, security can stop the truck at the gate.

When you book with us, we tell you what your building is likely to ask for. We plan the move around your lift slot and send enough people to finish inside it. We also wrap furniture properly so it doesn't knock the lift walls or corridors on the way out.

In summer we start early in the morning. It's easier on your things, and on our crew.

## Questions people ask about moving in Dubai

### How much do movers and packers in Dubai cost?
A studio starts from AED 750, a 1 BHK from AED 1,100 and a 3 bedroom villa from AED 3,000. The final price depends on how much you have and the access at both buildings. We confirm a fixed price before the move.

### How soon can you move me?
Often the same day or the next day, if we have a free truck. The last week of the month is busy in Dubai because many leases end then, so book three to five days ahead if you can.

### Do you work on Fridays and at night?
Yes. We're open 24/7, including Fridays and public holidays. Night moves are popular for offices and for buildings that limit daytime moves.

### Do you bring boxes and packing material?
Yes. Boxes, bubble wrap, stretch film and blankets are included in the price.

### What happens if something gets damaged?
Our moves are insured. If anything is damaged while we're moving it, we'll repair it, replace it, or compensate you.

### Do I need a permit to move in Dubai?
Most towers and many communities ask for a move-out or move-in permit and a service lift booking. Contact your building management a few days before the move. We'll tell you what they usually need.

### How long does a move take?
A studio or 1 BHK usually takes half a day. A 2 or 3 BHK takes about a day. Villas take one to two days, depending on size.

### Can you move me from Dubai to another emirate?
Yes. We move to and from all seven emirates, and to other GCC countries.

### How do I pay?
Cash, card or bank transfer.
