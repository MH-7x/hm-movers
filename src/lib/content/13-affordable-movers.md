## How to make your move cheaper

**Move in the middle of the month.** The last week of the month is the busiest, because many leases end then. Mid-month dates are easier to book.

**Pick a weekday.** Weekends fill up first.

**Take less with you.** Sell or give away furniture you won't use. Fewer items means a smaller truck and a lower price.

**Pack what you can.** Clothes, books and linen are easy to pack yourself. Leave the kitchen and fragile things to us if you're worried about breakages.

**Get your building ready.** Book the service lift and get your permit sorted early. Waiting at the gate wastes time on both sides.

**Tell us everything when you call.** The more accurate the details, the more accurate the price. Surprises on the day are what push prices up.
