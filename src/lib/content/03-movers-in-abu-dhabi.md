## Planning an Abu Dhabi move: what to sort out first

**Building and community permits.** Many towers on Reem Island and Al Raha Beach, and many gated villa communities, ask for a move permit and a booked service lift or gate pass. Ask your building or community management a few days before the move. We'll tell you what they usually need.

**Truck timing.** Abu Dhabi limits heavy trucks on some main roads during rush hours. We schedule our trucks around those times so your move doesn't get stuck on the road.

**Villa moves take planning.** A 5 bedroom villa in Khalifa City is a very different job from a 1 BHK in Al Khalidiyah. For bigger homes we visit first, check the access, and agree a crew size and timing with you.

**Summer heat.** From June to September we start early in the morning. It's better for your furniture, your electronics and our crew.
